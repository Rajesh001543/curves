Size Guide

Find your perfect fit. Our garments are designed to flatter, but knowing your exact measurements ensures a flawless look every time.

How to Measure

1

Bust

Measure under your arms at the fullest part of your bust. Keep the tape level.

2

Waist

Measure around your natural waistline, keeping the tape comfortably loose.

3

Hips

Stand with your feet together and measure around the fullest part of your hips.
