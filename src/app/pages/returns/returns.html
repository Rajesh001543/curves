How it works

1. Initiate

Enter your order details to start. Select the items you want to return and tell us why.

2. Print Label

Instantly receive a prepaid return shipping label via email. Print it out and attach it to your package.

3. Pack & Ship

Securely pack your items in their original packaging and drop the box off at any authorized carrier location.

4. Refund

Once we receive and inspect your items, your refund will be processed within 3-5 business days.

Return Policy Highlights

30-Day Window

We accept returns on all unworn, unwashed items with tags still attached within 30 days of the original delivery date.

Free Store Credit Returns

Returns for store credit or exchanges are completely free. Refunds to the original payment method will incur a ₹150 restocking fee.

Final Sale Items

Items marked as "Final Sale" cannot be returned or exchanged. Intimates, swimwear, and pierced jewelry are also non-returnable for hygiene reasons.

International Returns

Currently, we do not offer prepaid return labels for international orders. International customers are responsible for return shipping costs.
